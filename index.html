Straight answers

Before you spend anything.

The diagnosis exists to reduce uncertainty, including the possibility that the right answer is not to build.

What if the diagnosis says I do not need what I asked for?

I say so. You still keep the written finding and the practical next step. The $150 buys the answer, not a commitment to hire me.

Do I pay for the whole website upfront?

No. The only service you book and pay for directly here is the $150 diagnostic. After I inspect your needs and we agree on scope, I send a separate payment request for 50% to begin. The remaining 50% is due when the work is complete.

Do I own the finished work?

Yes. The code, accounts, domain, and operating documentation are yours. You receive a walkthrough at handoff.

Is it safe to let an agent act for my business?

I use explicit permissions, approval gates, spending caps, a kill switch, an append-only action log, and a separate checker. I do not ship unattended money-spending autonomy.

Is ongoing support required?

No. Ongoing tuning, monitoring, and reporting are available and scoped after the build. You keep the agent, code, and accounts either way.

How much work will setup create for me?

The onboarding promise is one 20-minute call. I drive; you log in only when I point at a screen and review the working result before release.

Can you work with my current platform?

Often, yes. I have worked across custom code and no-code systems. The diagnosis determines whether a direct repair, integration, or migration is the honest path.
