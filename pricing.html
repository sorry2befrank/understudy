Know the budget before we talk.

These build prices are planning ranges, not checkout prices or a quote for an unseen project. For a new build, book the $500 consultation first. To fix something you already have, book the $150 diagnostic. Once we agree on scope, I request 50% to begin and the remaining 50% when the work is complete.
