Diagnostic, consultation and build pricing

Know the budget before we talk.

Every build or fix starts with the $150 diagnostic. After I see what you need, I give you a fixed price; these are not checkout prices. Once we agree on scope, I request 50% to begin and the remaining 50% when the work is complete.
