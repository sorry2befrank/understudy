New to AI agents? Here's the plain version.

Think of an agent as an assistant that learns one job and does it for you — drafting your posts, handling routine replies, sorting your data, sending the follow-ups. The difference from "AI that just chats": an agent actually does the work.

And you're always the boss. It checks with you before anything important, keeps a record of everything it does, and you can stop it any time. You stay the lead — the understudy just makes sure the show goes on.
