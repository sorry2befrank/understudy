What happens before we automate anything.

  1. Inspect the current workflow. Show me the repeated task, the tools involved and the points where work gets delayed. Do not send passwords or customer records through the public inquiry form.
  2. Get a written diagnosis. The $150 diagnosis produces a finding, a practical next step and a proposed scope. It is credited toward the work if we continue.
  3. Approve the build and its boundaries. Deliverables, access, recurring provider costs and pricing are agreed before work begins. We test on a preview or development copy where practical.
  4. Keep the system and the handoff. You receive the code, accounts and operating documentation. Ongoing support is optional and scoped separately.

I do not promise a percentage saving before seeing the process. We establish a baseline, then compare the finished workflow against it.
