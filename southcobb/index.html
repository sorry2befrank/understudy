Our Mission

Where Passion Meets Profession

The South Cobb High School Student Film Program is more than a class — it's a career launchpad. Under the direction of Aisha Davis, students gain real-world experience in every aspect of film production, from pitch decks to post-production.

Georgia has become one of the world's largest film production hubs, generating over $4 billion annually. Our program prepares students to step directly into this thriving industry.

  • Professional-grade equipment training
  • 140+ active students annually
  • Award-winning student productions
  • Georgia's #1 high school film program
